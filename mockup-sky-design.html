3 · The craft — every type read from real behavior

Earth — youThe wallet you flashed. You stand on your own money and look up.
SatelliteAn ordinary wallet in your orbit. Normal traffic, nothing unusual.
FreighterA relay. Money lands and leaves within minutes — hauling cargo onward to put distance behind it.
MothershipA collection hub — many small craft return here. When two of your own paths reconverge on one, that's a single operator.
UFOUnidentified. A contract or operator we can't name yet. Naming it is exactly what the paid report does.
Space stationAn institution — exchange, bank, brokerage. Keeps identity records and answers legal process. The actionable object.
DerelictDormant — nothing has moved in over a year. Parked, not gone. The sky notices the moment it wakes.
Cloaked craftA mixer or shuffler — high-frequency in-and-out designed to blur the trail. It rarely works; the chain still records every hop.
Why the taxonomy earns its keep: the shape is read from data the scanner already computes — direction, dwell time, volume share, dormancy, contract-vs-wallet, convergence. So a 68-year-old can look at her own sky and understand it without knowing what a smart contract is. Kwong's sky reads in one glance: a UFO feeding Earth, three freighters hauling $602k away, two of them meeting at one mothership.
What this replaces: the planet-lore system (Planet Walker, resident creatures, "shrouded worlds"). A ruler of a world doesn't survive the shift to craft — and the forensic voice we moved to already fits the new spine. Nothing else changes: same engine, same data, same 3D sky.
